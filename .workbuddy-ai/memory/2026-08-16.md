## CourseDetailsView.tsx — refactor ChapterRow action buttons

Replaced the tangled truth-table in `components/student/CourseDetailsView.tsx` (the
`ChapterRow` component) with two simple per-content state machines:

```ts
videoButton: "watch" | "activate" | "hidden"
pdfButton:   "open"  | "activate" | "hidden"
```

Inputs are `chapter.is_locked`, `chapter.can_watch`,
`is_free_preview` (video), `is_free_preview_attachment` (PDF),
`hasPdf`, `pdfUrl`, and the existing helpers (`isStudentChapterVideoPlayable`,
`isStudentChapterVideoRequiresActivation`, `isStudentChapterPdfVisible`,
`isStudentChapterPdfRequiresActivation`, plus `isNoVideoUrl` for placeholder
detection).

Render rules:
- `chapterLocked` → one gray "Activate Chapter" button.
- `viewsExhausted` → one red "Re-activate" button.
- otherwise → render `videoButton` (if any) then `pdfButton` (if any).
  Free preview on video → user opens video only. Free preview on attachment
  → user opens PDF only. Mixed = up to two buttons with per-type activation.

Why it matters: the previous `bothFreePreviewDisabled` shortcut collapsed two
independent activation paths into one button, hiding paid PDF behind the same
activation as paid video even when one of them was a free preview.

Helpers used (already imported):
- `@/src/lib/student-chapter-access`: `isNoVideoUrl`, plus the four
  `isStudentChapter*` access helpers.
- The cast-based `(attrs.is_free_preview as number | boolean) === 1 || === true`
  was removed — kept `attrs.is_free_preview` as-is since the helpers already
  normalise via `coercePreviewFlag`.

Lint notes: existing `ExamsTab` useMemo at line 1217 already had a
pre-existing `react-hooks/exhaustive-deps` warning (it references
`lockedCourse` but only lists `[exams]`). Not touched — out of scope.
